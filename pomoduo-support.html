Contact support

PomoDuo is developed by Paul Khayet. For questions, bug reports, or feedback, email:

support@thesimplestcalorietracker.com

This is our shared support inbox. Please include PomoDuo in the subject line so we can identify your app.

What to include

  • Your device model and iOS version.
  • Your PomoDuo version, shown in the app's Settings under About.
  • What happened, what you expected, and the steps that led to the issue.
  • For timer or alert problems, whether the app was open, in the background, or the device was locked.
  • A screenshot if it helps, with any personal task details hidden.

We cannot access your tasks or focus history remotely. Only send information you are comfortable sharing.

Common questions

How do focus sessions and breaks work?

Choose a task, start a focus session, and take a short break when it finishes. After your chosen number of completed focus sessions, take a longer break. Each interval waits for you to start it. Change focus and break durations in Settings.

Will the timer continue when I lock my device?

A running timer continues when you switch apps or lock your device. Use Pause to preserve the remaining time, or Reset to stop the cycle. Use those controls to stop a session instead of force-closing the app.

Why didn't I hear an alert?

Check PomoDuo's sound settings and its alarm and notification permissions in iOS Settings. Allow alarms for an alert that can sound through Silent mode and Focus. If alarm access is unavailable, standard notifications follow your device's notification, sound, and Focus settings.

If the problem continues, email support with the interval length and whether the device was locked or the app was in the background.

Why didn't a skipped session count toward my progress?

Only completed focus sessions earn focus time. Skipped and reset intervals do not count toward completed focus time. Timer options let you restart an interval, skip ahead, or reset a cycle.

How can I export or clear my session history?

Open Insights and choose “Export history as CSV” to save completed sessions using the system file picker. Choose “Clear history” in Insights to remove completed session history. You can also delete individual tasks from your task list.

Clear history is permanent within the app. Export anything you want to keep first.

Where is my data stored? Can support restore it?

Tasks, completed sessions, timer state, and preferences are stored on your device. Depending on your settings, iOS may include them in device backups. PomoDuo does not have an account or app-managed cloud sync, and support cannot access or restore your local data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Are tips required? What if a payment has a problem?

Tips are optional and unlock no extra features. Apple handles payments through the App Store. A pending tip may require approval; if you were charged and need billing or refund help, use Apple's Report a Problem service. For an issue with the tip screen itself, email us.
