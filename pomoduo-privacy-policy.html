PomoDuo for iOS

Privacy Policy

Your focus stays yours. This policy explains how PomoDuo, developed by Paul Khayet, handles information when you use the app or contact support.

Effective date: October 26, 2026

At a glance

PomoDuo does not collect your tasks or focus history. The app works without an account and has no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. Your tasks, completed sessions, timer state, and preferences are stored locally on your device, and are not sent to us.

Information stored on your device

PomoDuo saves the tasks you create, completed focus sessions and their associated task information, the current timer state, and preferences such as interval lengths, sounds, and appearance. The app uses this information to run timers, display your task list, and show your progress and session history.

We do not operate an account or cloud service that receives this information. We cannot view or retrieve your local tasks, sessions, or settings.

Alarms, notifications, and Live Activities

PomoDuo uses Apple system features, including AlarmKit, local notifications, and Live Activities, to show timer progress and alert you when an interval ends. These features are handled by iOS. Task titles are not included in alarm presentations or Live Activities.

You control permissions for alarms, notifications, and Live Activities through iOS settings. These permissions do not give us access to your tasks or focus history.

Backups, exports, and your controls

Depending on your device backup settings, iOS may include PomoDuo data in an iCloud or computer backup. Those backups are managed by you and Apple; we cannot access them. This is device backup behavior, rather than a PomoDuo account or app-managed cloud sync service.

  • You can delete individual tasks in the task list and clear completed session history from Insights.
  • You can export completed sessions as a CSV file from Insights. The system file picker saves the export to the destination you choose. If you choose a cloud storage provider or share the file, that service's privacy practices apply.
  • Deleting the app removes its local data. Offloading the app may retain data. Previously created backups and exported files are managed separately through your device, backup, or storage settings.

Because we do not have your local data, we cannot delete it remotely or restore it for you.

Optional tips and Apple services

You can optionally support PomoDuo with a tip through Apple's in-app purchase system. Tips do not unlock extra features. Apple processes the payment; PomoDuo receives transaction information needed to verify and complete the tip, but does not receive your card details or billing address.

Apple handles App Store transactions and device services under its own Privacy Policy.

When you contact support

If you email support@thesimplestcalorietracker.com, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. This inbox is shared with our other app, so please mention PomoDuo in the subject line.

We use that information to respond to your request and troubleshoot problems. Support messages are processed by our email service provider and retained as needed to handle the request, related follow-up, and any applicable legal obligations. Please avoid sending private task details or other sensitive information unless necessary.

You can contact us about information you have provided by email, including a request to delete support correspondence, subject to any retention obligations. This does not give us access to data stored within the app.

Sharing and tracking

We do not sell or rent personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or track you across apps and websites. PomoDuo does not send your task or session information to third parties. Apple services, destinations you choose for exports, and support email are described above.

Children's privacy

PomoDuo does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Its tasks and timer history stay on the device. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support inbox, contact us so we can address it.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as PomoDuo changes. The effective date above identifies the latest version. Any questions about PomoDuo or this policy can be sent to Paul Khayet at support@thesimplestcalorietracker.com.
